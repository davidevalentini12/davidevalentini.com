How I work

01 — Analysis

Strategy first

Before any visual or written output, I get to know the business. Who you are, who your clients are, how you sit against competitors, what makes you different. That is the brand audit: a strategic document that guides everything that follows.

02 — Identity

Messages and voice

Values, tone of voice, key messages and visual identity all derive from the positioning. Every visual and verbal choice serves the competitive difference we want to communicate.

03 — Execution

A coherent presence

Website, ads, content, campaigns. All built on the strategy we define together. The result is a digital presence that holds together across every touchpoint — instead of a set of disconnected pieces.

"Strategy only works when the people who design it and the people who execute it are aligned. That is why I prefer to follow the whole process, from research to execution."

Who I work
best with

I do not work for everyone. I work best with people who have a quality product or service and find themselves running communication without a dedicated team — or with a team that is not delivering the results they expected.

  • Italian B2B SMEs, 5–30 employees Mostly IT and professional services: consulting, law and accounting firms, architecture, engineering. Companies with a solid offer and a digital presence that does not reflect it.
  • Founders and CEOs who run communication themselves They know what they want to say, but not how to say it so that it lands. Or they do not have the time to do it properly.
  • Anyone who wants a strategy before investing in ads Promoting something that is badly positioned is burning budget. The brand audit is the right place to start.

Davide Valentini

I am a communication strategist, trained in design at Politecnico di Milano. I work on what comes first: before the LinkedIn post, before the website, before the ad copy.

Who are we talking to? What do they need? How do we reach them? In what language? Answering those questions well is the difference between communication that works and noise.

"For years I thought that not having a single vertical skill was my limitation. Then I realised the strategic overview was my skill."

I have worked on brand strategy, digital strategy, political communication and ad campaigns — always with the same logic: understand the market, define the difference, build a coherent presence. That systemic versatility is what works for companies with no dedicated communication team, who cannot afford several suppliers who never talk to each other.
